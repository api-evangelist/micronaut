The Micronaut Foundation is excited to announce the release of Micronaut framework 4.10.14! It contains releases of Micronaut Core and Micronaut Data. We strongly recommend that users update to Micronaut 4.10.14, as it ships with Netty 4.2.13, which fixes multiple CVEs.
