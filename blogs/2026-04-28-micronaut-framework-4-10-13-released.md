The Micronaut Foundation is excited to announce the release of Micronaut framework 4.10.13! It contains releases of Micronaut Core, Micronaut Sourcegen, and Micronaut SQL. The latest version of Micronaut Core, included in this release, contains fixes for two security advisories related to unbounded caches in TimeConverterRegistrar and ResourceBundleMessageSource.
