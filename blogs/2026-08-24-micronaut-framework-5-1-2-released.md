The Micronaut Foundation is excited to announce the release of Micronaut framework 5.1.2! It includes a release of Micronaut Core, Cache, Test, Data, Security, Serialization, and Flyway. If you haven’t yet updated to Micronaut Framework 5, this is an excellent opportunity to do so!
